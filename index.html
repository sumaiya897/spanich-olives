The Perelló story

A name, a market stall, and an olive worth
the fuss.

Few brands can point to one object and say: that is us. Ours is a large green gordal olive, drawn once in 1964 and kept ever since.

  1. 1964

    Gaspar names the olive.

    A young Gaspar Perelló builds his business and his brand on his own surname, with the simple image of a single large green olive: the gordal. He trained in Alcalá de Guadaíra, learning how to choose olives the southern Spanish way.

  2. The stand

    Barcelona's Boqueria.

    From an olive curing and packing facility, Gaspar supplies the Boqueria market with olives and pickles: gordal, manzanilla, empeltre and arbequina, north and south of Spain on one counter. His wife and partner, María Ribó, works beside him.

  3. Early 1990s

    A stall worth a detour.

    Monika Linton of Brindisa, searching for real Spanish food to bring to London, finds the Perelló stand. Three decades of working together follow.

  4. Today

    Same olive, bigger table.

    When Gaspar and María retire, Brindisa takes up stewardship of the brand. The range grows to pickles, nuts, snacks, olive oil crackers and pulses, with the same promise: carefully chosen, cooked to perfection, never overcomplicated.
